The Big Eye Support

We can help with gameplay, purchases, restore problems, privacy, accessibility, and technical issues.

Contact

Email kolequant@gmail.com.

Include your device model, Android or iOS version, app version, and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. Try the in-game Restore Purchases control while signed into the store account that made the purchase. Billing disputes and refunds are handled under the applicable store’s rules.

Location permission

Location is optional and affects only ambient weather personalization. You can deny or revoke it in device settings without blocking gameplay.

Local data

The current release stores game progress locally and does not require an account. Uninstalling or resetting the game may remove local progress. Back up anything important using the options available on your device.

Response time

We aim to review support messages within five business days.
