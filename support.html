The Big Eye Support

We can help with gameplay, purchases, restore problems, privacy, accessibility, and technical issues.

Contact

Email kolequant@gmail.com.

Include your device model, Android or iOS version, app version, and a short description. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. Try the in-game Restore Purchases control while signed into the store account that made the purchase. Billing disputes and refunds are handled under the applicable store’s rules.

Location permission

Location is optional and affects only ambient weather personalization. You can deny or revoke it in device settings without blocking gameplay.

Local data

The current release stores game progress locally and does not require an account. Uninstalling or resetting the game may remove local progress. Back up anything important using the options available on your device.

Delete The Big Eye data

To delete a profile in The Big Eye, open the profile picker, choose Delete profile for that profile, then confirm. The app removes the local profile and its saved games. When it reconnects, it requests removal of that installation’s cloud profile backup, saved email preferences, and linked gameplay activity.

To request deletion from outside the app, email kolequant@gmail.com with the subject “The Big Eye data deletion”. Tell us which profile or guest installation the request concerns and what you want removed. We may ask for information to verify the request or identify the records. Keep passwords and store credentials private.

Deleting a profile does not cancel store purchases or delete Apple’s or Google’s purchase records. Guest activity and records from an earlier installation may need a separate request. Data that must be kept for legal obligations is retained only as required. See the privacy policy for the data collected.

Response time

We aim to review support messages within five business days.
