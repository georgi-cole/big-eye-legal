The Big Eye Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains how The Big Eye handles information when you use the mobile game.

Information stored on your device

The game stores settings, player profiles, optional profile images, game progress, season history, and local conversation history on your device. This information is used to save your experience and is not transmitted merely because it is stored locally. You can remove it using available reset controls or by uninstalling the app.

Profile backup and gameplay analytics

When the online backend is enabled for this version, saved profiles are automatically backed up to Supabase. The backup includes the profile identifier, display name, avatar, selected appearance, achievements, and unlock progress. Photos, biographies, wallet data, season archives, and saved games remain on the device. The backup uses an anonymous account stored by this installation. Adding an optional contact email does not change this identity or provide restoration of device-only data on another device.

The app automatically sends pseudonymous gameplay events to Supabase, including guest activity. Saved-profile events include the installation-local profile ID, selected appearance, screen visits and visible time, game modes and numbered days reached, minigame duration, placements and exits, Social module use and action categories, selected gameplay decisions, elimination timing and season placement, marketplace product choices and purchase outcomes, and session duration. Purchase prices are not collected. The profile ID lets the project owner join gameplay events to that profile's backup and display name. Analytics uses a separate anonymous installation identifier. Session events also include broad device type and family, operating system, and browser; an exact device model is included only when the browser provides it. A full browser user-agent string is not sent. Analytics does not include biographies or conversation text. Events carry a build label (QA, production, or development) so test activity can be distinguished. Guest events have no profile ID and are grouped by the anonymous installation identifier. Guest history is not retroactively merged into a newly created profile. Activity groups describe records, not a verified count of unique people. Deleting a profile on its original installation queues removal of its online backup, contact details, and linked analytics. The app must reconnect to complete the removal. Other analytics rows remain until removed by the project owner or in response to a deletion request. For access or deletion requests, email kolequant@gmail.com.

Optional profile email and game updates

When this feature is enabled, you may add an email while creating or editing a profile. Supabase stores it in a private contact record linked to that profile so the project owner can associate your contact information and communication preference with your player record. The address is unverified and is not used to sign in, recover saves, or automatically combine profiles. It is not copied into local game saves, profile backup snapshots, or gameplay event payloads. Authorized administrators can view it with your preference and broad activity groups, such as returning or inactive player records.

The optional, initially unchecked subscription box gives consent to receive The Big Eye news and playtest invitations by email. Entering an address or accepting this policy does not subscribe you. We record your choice, the displayed consent version, and when the choice changed. Invitations do not automatically enroll you in a testing program. You can turn off this profile's subscription in Edit Profile. Each campaign email must also include an unsubscribe link that stops these messages for the address, without requiring a game login or deleting your profiles. Changing the address requires a new subscription choice.

Your contact address remains linked to the profile until you clear it in Edit Profile or the profile's online record is deleted. Clearing the address also removes its subscription. Deleting a profile requests deletion of its contact record with its backup when the device next connects. Uninstalling alone does not delete online records. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact kolequant@gmail.com.

Optional location

Location starts off and is unavailable for teen or unknown-age players. An adult must enable weather location in Player privacy & family and grant device permission before coordinates can be requested.

If you grant location access, the app may send coordinates to Open-Meteo to request current weather and select a matching ambient visual theme. Location is optional, is not required to play, and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not intentionally retain coordinates after the request.

Diary Room responses

The release processes Diary Room responses on your device. Diary Room text is not sent to the developer or an AI provider in this release. Do not submit sensitive personal information. If a future version enables online responses, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases and advertising

Optional, one-time digital purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The game reads purchase status from the store to unlock owned features and support purchase restoration. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Supported mobile builds include Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising is optional and requires an adult player's separate choice in Player privacy & family. Where required, UMP presents Google's privacy and consent choices before live ad requests. When advertising is enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share the device's IP address, device or advertising identifiers, advertising data (such as ads shown), app and ad interactions (such as taps or video views), crash diagnostics, and performance information. IP address may be used to estimate a device's general location. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and SDK performance. The app requests non-personalized ads; this does not mean the SDK processes no data. You can turn optional advertising off and revisit advertising privacy choices in Settings → Player privacy & family. Test builds can use Google's sample ads. No Ads and VIP remove automatic ad breaks; voluntary rewarded ads remain available by choice. See Google's advertising privacy information and how Google uses data when you use partners' apps or sites.

Sharing and sale

Optional usage measurement starts off and is unavailable for minors. If an adult enables it and a measurement service is configured, screen and game events with a session identifier may be sent to that service. Profile text and photos are not part of these events. Switching it off stops new events and removes the local event buffer. The game can retrieve static content and configuration after admission; hosting providers receive ordinary network metadata.

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers when needed to deliver an optional feature, comply with law, protect users, or maintain the service. Google's handling of advertising data is described above and in Google's linked privacy information.

Children

This version defaults to a minimum player age of 18 while its content rating is reviewed. This is a product access policy, not an assigned store rating. The teen permission path described below applies only to a reviewed version whose minimum age permits it. Parent permission cannot override a minimum player age or applicable content restriction.

The Big Eye is not directed to children under 13. Store age ratings and target-audience settings reflect the final game content and local requirements.

A neutral age question stores an age group and permission choices on this device. This is self-declaration, not identity verification. Play for players declaring an age under 13 remains locked because a verified parent-consent service is not connected. The parent screen sends no consent request or parent/child personal information. A higher store or local legal age restriction still applies.

Players aged 13–17 are asked to involve a parent or guardian in permission for local play. This acknowledgment is not verified parental consent for online processing. Optional location, usage measurement, online Diary Room/result services, advertising, purchases, and restore requests are unavailable for teen players. Adults can choose optional features separately and withdraw choices in Settings → Player privacy & family. Enabling a setting does not activate an unavailable service.

Stored or newly entered younger profile ages can tighten restrictions. Switching profiles or guest mode does not lift them. Profile deletion is available even on the locked screen under Privacy, saved data, and help. Parent help and genuine age corrections can be discussed with support; do not send a child’s personal information with an initial inquiry. Age corrections cannot currently unlock play through this app.

Security, retention, and choices

Network services use HTTPS. You may deny or revoke location permission. Local game data remains until reset or uninstall. For privacy or deletion requests, email kolequant@gmail.com or visit the official support page.

Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers, or legal requirements change. The date above identifies the latest version.
