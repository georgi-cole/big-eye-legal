Purchases & Refunds

Last updated 22 November 2026

This page explains real-money purchases, delivery, restoration and refund requests for Crystal Path: Infinity. It complements our Terms & Conditions and does not replace statutory consumer rights.

Where purchases are made What you receive Delivery and restoration Refund requests Your consumer rights Rewarded ads Help and account deletion

Where and how you buy

Real-money in-app purchases, when available, use the App Store on iPhone and Google Play on Android. The platform's confirmation screen shows the actual product, currency, total price, applicable taxes and any recurring charge before you authorize payment. Please review those details before confirming. The public browser game has no real-money checkout; any purchase preview there does not complete a payment.

Product availability, prices and promotions may vary by country, platform and time. The offer shown in the app and the store checkout explains the product being sold; an item shown as unavailable cannot be purchased through this page.

What digital items provide

Products may include SP, hints, boosts, characters, equipment, Worlds or other game content. Each product's in-game description specifies its quantity, permitted use, duration if relevant, and whether it is consumed through gameplay. Virtual currency and items cannot be exchanged for real money or guaranteed competitive results.

A verified store transaction is required before a paid entitlement is credited. Some items are consumed when used, while others remain available according to their offer. Content and purchases can be associated with the eligible signed-in game account; do not assume an entitlement transfers between different game accounts, Apple and Google stores, or platforms.

Missing items and Restore Purchases

If a successful purchase does not appear, allow the store and game to reconnect, then use Restore Purchases in the app where available. Use the same store account that completed the transaction and, where relevant, the game account to which the purchase belongs. Some purchases may take time to verify or reconcile. Restoration depends on product type and purchase status: previously consumed items are not necessarily reissued as fresh balances.

A purchase may fail or remain unavailable if the platform, network or verification service is unreachable. Do not pay again just to resolve a missing item. Contact support with the product name, store, approximate purchase time and the store order reference if available. Do not send payment-card details, passwords or sign-in codes.

Requesting a refund

Apple App Store: use Report a Problem or read Apple's refund instructions. Eligibility and processing are determined according to the applicable store rules and law.

Google Play: use Google Play's refund request instructions. If you are in the EEA or UK, also see the regional refund guidance. You can contact us about delivery failures or rights under applicable law.

If the store refunds, cancels or revokes a transaction, the associated entitlement or unused balance may be removed or adjusted in accordance with the product and purchase status. We do not treat every purchase as automatically non-refundable.

Statutory rights remain

Consumer rights differ by country and transaction. In the EU and EEA, applicable rights concerning digital content and services can include remedies if the item is not delivered, is defective or does not match its description. Any statutory withdrawal right, and any conditions affecting it for immediately supplied digital content, depend on applicable law and the checkout process. Neither this page nor a store policy removes mandatory rights you may have.

For a malfunction or an unmet entitlement, please contact support@kolequant.xyz so we can investigate alongside the relevant store process.

Rewarded ads are not purchases

Watching an optional rewarded ad, when available, does not charge a purchase price. Ad inventory is not guaranteed; verified completion may be required before granting a continuation or reward. Limits and exact reward amounts are shown in the applicable game screen.

Support, privacy and deletion

For purchase issues, email support@kolequant.xyz or visit support. For account deletion, use the account-deletion request instructions. Some transaction records may be retained where required by law; read the privacy policy.
